## Interpretation controls

- Numeric and deadline rules are automated where the source is explicit.
- Rules depending on establishment type, ministerial decisions, occupation, or fact-specific legal judgment remain review controls with evidence and owner fields.
- Product copy says “requires review” when the system cannot safely make the legal determination.
- The attached regulation remains the controlling source for this release; future legal updates require a new effective-dated matrix version and regression run.
